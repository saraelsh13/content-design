Developmental edit · Line edit · Coaching

From first draft to launch copy

An editorial exercise using a simulated draft and public source material for Compass Video Intelligence.

What’s being edited and why

Cohere’s Compass helps organizations search their own information. This exercise takes a fictional launch draft about video search and makes its business value concrete. It also shows how I’d verify what buyers can access before publishing.

Start with the Cohere overview and journey map

Developmental edit

Simulated draft opening
Compass unlocks every moment in your video library at scale. All teams can instantly find the exact answer in any recording. It’s available now for enterprises.
Revised opening
The rollout date moved during a planning call. The brief still shows the old date. Somewhere in the recording, the team agreed on a new one and explained what had to happen first.

Note to the writer. You’ve identified the useful shift from retrieving a file to retrieving a moment. Let’s lead with a problem the reader recognizes. Then show what comes back in a result and how the reader can check it. Close with design-partner access. On the next pass, can you give us one example where the information on screen matters as much as what’s said?

Line edit and fact-check

Draft phraseReplacementEditorial decision
Unlocks every momentReturns timestamped clips for relevant momentsName the result. The source describes retrieval behavior, with no basis here for exhaustive coverage.
All teams can instantly find the exact answerUsers can review a clip, transcript, and visual description from recordings they’re authorized to accessRemove unsupported speed and accuracy claims. Bring the permission scope into the sentence.
Available now for enterprisesCohere is recruiting design partnersUse the status in the announcement and direct interested readers to the registration path.

What I’d clear before publication

Engineering reviews how clip boundaries and permissions work. Product Marketing verifies the program status and CTA. The editor checks that the launch article, social copy, and linked product page use the same availability language. Any performance number needs its metric, comparison, and evaluation scope in either the copy or the linked evidence.
