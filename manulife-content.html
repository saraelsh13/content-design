Project overview · Manulife

Banking, AI conversations, and public web

I led content design for digital banking journeys and adapted the guidance to where customers encountered it, from the public website to a conversation or an authenticated task.

Digital banking

I worked on mortgage prepayments, sub-account flows, and dashboard journeys. I mapped the decisions and states behind the interface, then worked with Product, Design, Engineering, Accessibility, and Legal to make the copy clear about requirements, progress, and next steps.

See the mortgage prepayment flow

Conversational content for AI assistance

I worked on conversational content for an AI assistant in digital banking. I approached the conversation as part of the customer’s journey. What can the assistant help with at this point, and how should it direct the customer when they need to act elsewhere?

Public website with Marketing

I collaborated with Marketing on content for Manulife’s public website and on user research and testing. I worked across the customer-facing language that helps people understand an offering and the product content that helps them act on it.

From one decision to a shared standard

I led content strategy for the content design team across Bank and Insurance. I developed the team’s content style guide, and we used it and the content guidance for design-system components in our day-to-day work across both businesses.

I established a decision log to bring what we learned from testing and research back into the guide.
