Cohere editorial case study

One edit, from brief to handoff

A short Rerank 5 example that shows how I turn a launch claim into a useful developer decision.

ProcessSource audit Mini style guideGlossary Worked edit

Start with the editorial problem

The launch emphasizes the benefits of sending less irrelevant text downstream. The reader has to decide whether to change their pipeline. I’d structure the guide around what the feature returns, what happens without a qualifying span, and which measures tell the reader whether the change helped.

Practice draft

Context Focus automatically extracts the right passages and cuts token costs while keeping answer quality high. Turn it on to send only the best text to your model.

Edited copy

Enable Context Focus in a Rerank 5 request to get character offsets for passages that meet your threshold. If no passage qualifies, that result omits the span field. Decide whether your application should send more surrounding text, use the full document, or skip the result. Compare token use, retrieval latency, and whether both approaches preserve the details your answers need.

Note to the writer. The cost angle gives the piece a reason to exist. Lead with the decision a developer has to make, then move the missing-span behavior into the main example. Keep the savings claim conditional until we have a like-for-like comparison on the target corpus.

Release handoff

After technical review, I’d verify terminology against the glossary, add links to the canonical docs and methodology, and brief the distribution copy. I’d check recurring reader questions after release and reopen the guide if the default, supported surface, or response fields change.
