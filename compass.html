Product launch · Independent sample

Find the moment you need in a recording

Compass Video Intelligence brings timestamped clips into enterprise search. Cohere is recruiting design partners.

What this can change at work

A useful decision might be buried halfway through a recorded meeting. Cohere’s Compass is an enterprise search product. This independent launch sample shows how video search could help a team find the relevant moment and open the original recording to check it.

Start with the Cohere overview and journey map

The rollout date moved during a planning call. The brief still shows the old date. Somewhere in the recording, the team agreed on a new one and explained what had to happen first.

With Video Intelligence in Compass, an application can search meeting recordings and related documents together. It can return the relevant stretch of a recording with timestamps, a transcript, and a description of what was on screen. The person reading the result can open the original footage and check the context around the decision.

Search what people say and what they show

In an onboarding recording, a trainer might say “choose this option” while pointing at a setting. The transcript contains the instruction. The screen contains the name of the setting. Compass searches across both spoken and visual information, and identifies the relevant start and end times when it runs the search.

Bring video into the same search as documents

A result can surface a clip alongside a slide or document page. Compass applies access permissions to retrieval, so teams can build experiences that respect what each user can see. For a knowledge team, a useful pilot might start with a small training library and the questions employees already ask. Check whether the returned clip starts early enough to make sense and whether the person searching can open the original.

Work with Cohere as a design partner

If your organization has recordings people regularly need to revisit, see how to register interest in the design-partner program.
