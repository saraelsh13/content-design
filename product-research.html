Cohere product research

A map of Cohere’s products, what changed this fall, and questions an editor could help readers answer.

Reference topicLook up product roles, releases, and questions to check with their owners.

The product map in one question

Is the organization looking for an AI workspace staff can use, search it can add to its own software, or infrastructure for running AI models? Those needs lead to different Cohere products. This page goes deeper into what each product does and what an editor should verify before describing it.

How the products fit together

Start with what the reader is trying to do. They may want an enterprise workspace, a search and retrieval layer for their own application, or a dedicated environment to run models. Those questions lead to different starting points.

Product or model familyWhat it doesThe reader’s next question
NorthEnterprise AI workspace for agents, automations, skills, and libraries.Who sets up and maintains a reusable workflow, and what can each role see or change?
CompassSearch and retrieval platform for enterprise content. It also powers retrieval in North.Should we use North’s built-in experience or integrate Compass into an application of our own?
Model VaultDedicated inference for supported Cohere and third-party models, with a separate Encrypted offering.Which models and features are available in our region and offering?
CommandGenerative models for tasks such as answering questions and using tools. Command A+ is a named release in the family.Which model and deployment path meet the workflow’s quality, access, and cost requirements?
Embed and RerankEmbed represents content for search. Rerank orders candidates against a query and can return relevant spans with Context Focus.Where is our retrieval failing, and what do we change first?
Parse, Transcribe, North Small TranslateParse extracts structure from documents. Transcribe converts speech to text. North Small Translate is designed for machine translation.What does the model preserve? Which languages, inputs, licenses, and outputs apply to our workflow?
Cohere LabsResearch and open-science programs, including the multilingual Aya work.Which findings come from a research evaluation, and which capabilities can I deploy today?

What the editorial material already does well

It gives technical readers a next step. The Rerank release notes name the model identifiers, show a request, and state what an upgrade needs. An evaluative companion could help teams decide where to use spans and when to preserve more context.

It publishes measurable customer results. Ada’s case makes a split deployment legible through concrete measures. The editorial opportunity is to put the workload and environment right beside each result, especially when those figures are reused in social or sales content.

It explains why a model property matters. The CALIBER explainer distinguishes prediction before reasoning from assessment after reasoning. A follow-up could ask what information helps the person who receives the answer decide whether to check it.

It speaks to two readers from the same announcement. The video launch describes search results and a benchmark. A knowledge lead may need a pilot plan. A developer may need result fields and permission behavior. Give them a shared opening and distinct next steps.

Questions I’d take into interviews

  • Product and engineering. What do developers confuse most often across Compass, the model APIs, and North? Which constraints can we demonstrate in an example?
  • Customer team. Who made the deployment decision and what trade-off changed their mind? What evidence and language can the customer approve for reuse?
  • Security and product marketing. Where do deployment, geography, access, and retention claims change by offering? What canonical reference should marketing link to?
  • Cohere Labs. Which finding changes a practitioner’s decision today? What would be an appealing but unsupported interpretation?
  • Content strategy. Which search queries and customer questions show demand for a comparison, and which official page should own each answer?

Snapshot based on Cohere’s public announcements, product pages, release notes, and published customer material. Deployment and model availability should be rechecked before reuse.

Before using this research

Match the product to the reader’s job, then verify the specific model, access path, and deployment details in current documentation.
