How I’d edit for Cohere

Start with the reader’s decision

I traced three pieces backwards. For each, I recorded who it serves, what it asks the reader to believe or do, which source can support that claim, and what the reader still has to figure out. I used public discussion to spot questions worth investigating.

Why I follow Cohere

When I worked on drift metrics at IBM, I kept coming back to the question a reader might ask. What does it mean when this number moves? Cohere Labs’ work on reasoning confidence caught my attention for the same reason. An estimate can help, and there’s still a person who has to decide what to check next.
