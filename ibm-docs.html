Case study · IBM Data & AI

Documentation for the moments that matter

Technical writing across onboarding, administration, upgrades, model evaluation, and troubleshooting.

Start with the reader’s decision

When I worked on Drift v2 and multilingual metrics, the question that kept coming up for me was what the reader could do with a number. A definition had to tell them what it measured. The surrounding guidance had to help them decide what to check next.

I wrote usage guidance and metric references for watsonx.governance and Watson OpenScale. At IBM Software Hub and Cloud Pak for Data, I documented installation, upgrades, administration, known issues, and releases. I worked with engineers, product managers, and designers on updates including agentic evaluators.

Build maintenance into the work

I audited existing collections, piloted a way to flag pages for review, and developed templates, terminology libraries, and voice guidance. Working on both the UI and docs helped me spot when an explanation belonged in the product and when it needed a fuller reference.
