Case study · Manulife

What happens after I make the payment?

Making the rules for mortgage prepayments visible at the point where customers need them.

A small action with a lot behind it

From the customer’s side, it sounds simple. I want to put an extra payment toward my mortgage. The product has to account for eligibility, which account or subaccount receives the payment, the available prepayment limit, and when the payment is applied.

I led content design for the flow. I mapped the decision tree, wrote component-level language, and worked with product, engineering, design, accessibility, and legal to align terms and states.

Map decisions and system states side by side

Simplified reconstruction of the decision tree and content model from my portfolio.

Why the status mattered

A payment can be submitted before it is applied to the mortgage. That distinction shaped the confirmation copy and the reusable status language. It’s the kind of small wording decision that matters even more when the customer comes back later to check what happened.
